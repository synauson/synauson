# Upgrading to Synauson 1.8.0

What to change when you move a server, a gRPC client or a Java application from
Synauson 1.7 to 1.8.0. Licensing and operation are in
[`deployment.md`](../deployment.md).

## Server

- Pull `cr.synauson.com/synauson/synauson-server:1.8.0`; Java applications follow
  [Java (jsyn)](#java-jsyn). The models are the same as in 1.7.1, so nothing downloads
  again. The license key, flags and state directory carry over.
- Point readiness probes at `synauson.v1.Syn` instead of `synauson.v1.Synauson`.
  Liveness stays on `""`.
- A Free or expired license now runs up to 4 AI sessions (1.7.1 allowed 2) and at
  most 10 conferences at once across all your instances.
- An instance starts conferences only while it holds a share of the conference pool
  from `usage.synauson.com`; it gets one as it starts when it can reach the service.
  An unlimited conference pool and an air-gapped license need no share. A 1.7.1
  receipt carries no conference share, so an instance that first starts 1.8.0
  without reaching the service starts no conference, and reads `NOT_SERVING` for
  readiness, until a usage report gets through.
- In `--auth=token` mode, `--tls-cert` and `--tls-key` now turn on TLS, where 1.7
  ignored them, and setting only one stops the server from starting. If your
  token-mode config passes them, move its clients to TLS or drop both flags.
- `--offline` (jsyn `offline`) needs the usage receipt an online run keeps in the
  state directory, unless the license is air-gapped. Without one the server refuses
  to start. Run the instance once without `--offline` until a usage report gets
  through, then take it offline.

## License Agreement 1.13

1.8.0 ships the End User License Agreement 1.13
([full text](https://synauson.com/legal/eula/1.13/)); 1.7.1 shipped 1.11. What
changed for you:

- Conference Limit: a pool of concurrent conferences beside your AI sessions, 10 on
  the Free plan plus any you buy, with a burst on the bought part (25%, or what your
  order form states, at most 200%), or unlimited by order form. Running conferences
  never end because of it. Sustained use above it on a paid plan is billed at your
  per-conference rate, and an expired paid plan continues on the Free plan's 10
  (Sections 1.6, 1.7, 3, 9.7).
- Free plan: 4 AI sessions, up from 2 (Sections 1.27, 2.3).
- Developer Licenses: free, for developing, testing, staging and demos, not
  production, with 4 AI sessions and 10 conferences each, for one year and renewed on
  request. One that expires starts nothing new until renewed, and Synauson may
  revoke one used in production after 7 days' notice (Sections 2.8, 9.7).
- One Free plan license per organization: Synauson may treat everyone who requests
  keys from one email domain, other than public webmail, as one organization, and
  may revoke extra Free keys after 30 days' notice, letting you choose which to keep
  (Section 2.3).
- Report origins: the usage service keeps the public IP address each usage report
  comes from (an IPv6 address as its /64, up to a fixed number per license), its
  country and when it was first and last seen, for 90 days after last seen, only to
  detect misuse of Free and Developer licenses. The licensing server's request logs,
  which include the address, are kept at most 14 days (Section 12.7).
- Usage reports also carry conference counts and the instance's conference share
  (Section 12.3).
- Your content is never sent to Synauson, whatever the documentation or release notes
  say, except to a service Synauson offers separately that you choose through that
  service's own setting (Section 12.8, from 1.12).

## Java (jsyn)

Keep jsyn 1.6.0 on `jsyn-natives` 1.7.1 until jsyn 1.7.0 is released, then move both
together and follow jsyn's upgrade guide. Don't move the natives to 1.8.0 before
jsyn.

## gRPC clients

1.8.0 serves API 1.0.0 (`synauson.v1`), published as synauson/api 1.0.0 and
`synauson-api` 1.0.0 on PyPI (Python 3.11 or newer; the `status` extra adds what
reading `ErrorInfo` needs). Clients built from the 1.7 protos don't work with 1.8.0,
and 1.0.0 clients don't work with 1.7 servers, so move a client and its servers
together. Regenerate your client, then make these changes.

### Services and files

- `Synauson` is now two services. `ShutdownServer` and `GetResourceSnapshot` are on
  `SynAdmin`, in the new `admin.proto` (Python: `admin_pb2`, `admin_pb2_grpc`), with
  their messages. Everything else is on `Syn`.
- `GetConferenceState` is `GetConference`, and `UpdatePartyAudioConnections` is
  `SetAudioRoutes`; their messages are renamed to match, `AudioConnection` is
  `AudioRoute` and `connections` is `routes`.
- `ErrorReason` is in the new `errors.proto`.
- Java code is in `com.synauson.api.v1`, one top-level class per message. Go code is
  `github.com/synauson/api/gen/go/synauson/v1`.

### Types and numbers

- Strings from a fixed list are enums: license state, source and plan, model state,
  ONNX Runtime state, participant kind, pool level, usage contact, the STT and TTS
  pool states, every `source`, `limits_scope` and the cgroup version. Accept values
  you don't know: v1 can add them.
- `SessionUsage` is `PoolUsage` and its `level` the `PoolLevel` enum.
  `GetCapabilitiesResponse.sessions` and the new `conferences` (field 17, the
  license's conference pool) both use it.
- `LicenseInfo.plan` was the string `all` or `none` in field 8. It is now the
  `LicensePlan` enum in field 11, always set: `LICENSE_PLAN_FREE`, `_PAID`,
  `_DEVELOPER` or `_UNLICENSED`. Whether new work starts is `LicenseInfo.state`.
- `SpeakRelease` gained `SPEAK_RELEASE_NONE` = 1, so `FLUSH` is 2 and `END` is 3.
- Times are `google.protobuf.Timestamp`: `timestamp_unix_ms` is `event_time` (VAD,
  turn detection, transcript, file and agent events), `created_at_unix_ms` is
  `create_time`, `captured_at_unix_ms` is `capture_time`, `file_expiry` and
  `license_expiry` are `file_expire_time` and `license_expire_time`, `peak_at` is
  `peak_time`, and `last_contact`, `grace_ends`, `paused_since` and `refused_at` are
  `last_contact_time`, `grace_end_time`, `pause_time` and `refuse_time`.
- Every integer is signed; `days_remaining` is `int32`.
- New field numbers, which regenerating handles: the event cases of `VadEvent`,
  `TranscriptEvent` and `FileEvent`, `TurnDetectionEvent`'s `probability` and
  `turn_complete`, and `GetCapabilitiesResponse.usage_service`.
- `VadConfig`'s `threshold`, `min_silence_ms` and `min_speech_ms` and
  `TurnDetectionConfig.confidence_threshold` have presence: unset means the default
  (0.5, 300 ms, 100 ms, 0.5), where 1.7 used 0. Set `confidence_threshold` to 0 to
  keep ending turns at every pause.
- `TurnTranscript.probability` and the resource snapshot's memory and thread counts
  are unset when there is no value.

### Renamed fields

- `end_ms` (transcript deltas, turn transcripts) is `last_start_ms`, the same value.
- `opus_bitrate` is `opus_bitrate_bps`; `our_srtp_key` is `local_srtp_key`; `our_key`
  and `their_key` are `local_key` and `remote_key`; the remote media's `srtp_key` is
  `remote_srtp_key`.
- `LicenseInfo.name` is `display_name`, `DetectorTiming.ms` is `run_ms`, and
  `CgroupMemory.cgroup_version` is `version`.
- `WebRtcStats.jitter_ns` is `max_jitter_ns`, and `dtls_state` is `connection_state`.
- `ModelInfo.version` is `variant`, with the same values.
- `tokio_workers` and `tokio_blocking_workers` are `worker_threads` and
  `blocking_threads`.

### Removed fields

`SipStats.rtt_ms`, `DtmfEvent.duration_ms`, `SttCapacityInfo.calibrated`,
`CapabilityInfo.streams`, `GetCapabilitiesResponse`'s `ai_conferences` and old
`conferences` (field 4), and `AddRecordingParticipantRequest.detectors`. All were
always 0, ignored or retired.

### Errors and ids

- Match the `google.rpc.ErrorInfo` reason (domain `synauson.com`), never the status
  code or message. `ErrorReason` lists every reason.
- A license that starts nothing new is `FAILED_PRECONDITION` (`LICENSE_REJECTED`,
  `LICENSE_DISCONNECTED`, `LICENSE_EXPIRED`), not `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
- A model still downloading or loading is `UNAVAILABLE` (`MODEL_LOADING`).
- An instance with no share of a license pool yet is `UNAVAILABLE`
  (`LICENSE_SHARE_PENDING`), not `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`; retry later.
- A full conference pool is `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` (`CONFERENCE_POOL_LIMIT_REACHED`).
  `CONFERENCE_LIMIT_REACHED` is your own `--max-conferences` cap.
- A speaker with text-to-speech turned off is `FAILED_PRECONDITION`
  (`MODEL_UNAVAILABLE`), not `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`.
- The `STT_LAGGING` error event's metadata keys are `backlogMs` and `thresholdMs`.
- Ids are 1 to 128 characters from `A-Z`, `a-z`, `0-9`, `.`, `_` and `-`; others fail
  with `INVALID_ID`. A participant with text-to-speech and an id over 120 characters
  needs `tts.speaker_id`.
- `SetAudioRoutes` takes at most 4096 routes and `AddPriorityAudioFiles` 1 to 64
  files; a VAD `threshold` must be above 0 and below 1, and `min_silence_ms` and
  `min_speech_ms` 0 to 10000. Other values fail with `INVALID_ARGUMENT`.
